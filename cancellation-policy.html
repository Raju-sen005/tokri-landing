Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy describes how customers can request cancellation of a Tokri order.

1. Customer cancellation

Contact Tokri support as soon as possible with your order ID if you want to cancel. A cancellation request may be accepted before the store begins preparing or packing the order. Once preparation or dispatch has started, cancellation may not be possible.

2. Cancellation by Tokri or a store

An order may be cancelled if an item is unavailable, the service address is outside the active delivery area, payment cannot be confirmed, delivery cannot reasonably be completed, or a suspected error or misuse requires review. We will try to notify you when contact details are available.

3. Refund after cancellation

If an eligible prepaid order is cancelled and payment has been collected, the amount payable for the cancelled order will be refunded through the original payment method where supported. Bank/payment-provider processing times may vary.

4. Incorrect orders and changes

If you need to change an item, quantity or address, contact support promptly. Changes are subject to availability and whether the order has already been prepared or dispatched.

5. Contact

Email sensaab0005@gmail.com and include your order ID. Never share OTPs, passwords or payment PINs.
