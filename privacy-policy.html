Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tokri (“Tokri”, “we”, “our” or “us”) may collect, use, store and share information when you visit our website or use Tokri services in Gulabpura and Vijaynagar, Rajasthan.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address and delivery address.
  • Order details, items purchased, delivery instructions, and customer-support messages.
  • Basic technical information such as browser/device details, approximate location where enabled, and website usage data.
  • Payment status and transaction references from payment providers. We do not intend to store full card, UPI PIN or banking credentials.

2. How we use information

We use information to process and deliver orders, provide customer support, communicate order updates, improve our services, prevent fraud, maintain records, and comply with applicable law.

3. Sharing

We may share the minimum necessary information with local stores, delivery partners, payment providers, hosting/technology providers, and competent authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Location and permissions

If you allow location access, it may be used to check service availability and help with delivery. You can disable location permissions in your device settings; you may then need to enter your address manually.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed for service delivery, legal obligations, dispute resolution and legitimate business purposes. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements. You may also opt out of non-essential promotional messages where available.

7. Children

Tokri services are intended to be used by people able to place orders under applicable law. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and publish the revised version on this page. For privacy questions or requests, email sensaab0005@gmail.com.

This is a starter policy for the current website. Review it against Tokri’s actual data practices, vendor contracts and applicable Indian privacy requirements before launch.
