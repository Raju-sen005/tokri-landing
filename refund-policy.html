Refund Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy applies to orders placed through Tokri for delivery in the service areas currently supported by Tokri, including Gulabpura and Vijaynagar, Rajasthan.

1. When to request a refund

You may contact us if an order is not delivered, an item is missing, an item is damaged, or the delivered product materially differs from the order. For perishable or time-sensitive products, report the issue as soon as reasonably possible after delivery.

2. Review and resolution

We may ask for the order ID, a description of the issue and, where helpful, photographs of the item or packaging. We will review the information and may coordinate with the relevant store or delivery partner before deciding on a refund, replacement or other resolution.

3. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds will normally be returned to the original payment method where supported. Processing time depends on the payment provider or bank. For cash-on-delivery orders, we will confirm an appropriate refund method with you.

4. Non-refundable situations

Refunds may not be available for issues caused by incorrect delivery details supplied by the customer, or for a change of mind after a correctly delivered order, except where required by law or where Tokri confirms otherwise. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

5. How to contact us

Email sensaab0005@gmail.com with your order ID, registered phone number and issue details. Please do not send passwords, OTPs or payment PINs.

Final refund eligibility and timelines should be aligned with Tokri’s actual store, delivery and payment workflows before launch.
